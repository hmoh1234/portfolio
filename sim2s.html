Hate reading? Here’s the entire book in 3 minutes.

The Anthropocene Reviewed isn’t a normal novel with a villain and a sword fight. It’s 44 short, deeply personal essays where John Green reviews random parts of human life—from Diet Dr Pepper to Canada Geese—on a 5-star scale.

01

What is "The Anthropocene"?

It’s the current geological age where humans are the #1 force reshaping Planet Earth—from climate change to inventing air conditioning and internet memes.

02

Why a 5-Star Scale?

In the modern world, we rate everything (Uber rides, tacos, movies). John uses that goofy rating system as a trick to ask serious questions about grief, joy, and why life matters.

03

The Hidden "Plot"

Even without fictional chapters, the book follows John’s real-life battle with severe anxiety, depression, and the 2020 COVID-19 lockdown—moving from cynicism to hope.
